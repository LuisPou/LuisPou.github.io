<div class="slide slide-textphotos">
  <div class="slide-textphotos-text">
    <p>I had the opportunity to work as an apprentice at Berkeley Lab, where I got hands-on experience with programming, embedded systems, circuit design, microprocessors, sensors, and CAD, while contributing to real scientific research alongside the lab's scientists. This was a program called BLDAP!</p>

<div class="slide slide-textphotos">
  <div class="slide-textphotos-text">
    <p>We worked on many projects, my favorite being one where we programmed Arduinos, sensors, and actuators, and designed a home using OnShape CAD to be energy efficient using solar power.</p>
    <p>Other projects included analyzing large data sets and learning the fundamentals of machine learning (ML). Unfortunately, I don't have many pictures showcasing those projects.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="slide-textphotos-images">

<div class="slide slide-textphotos">
  <div class="slide-textphotos-text">
    <p>We also worked on things besides programming and hardware. In biology, we used a tool called an EcoFAB to observe the effects nitrogen had on plants.</p>
    <p>We grew two grass seeds, one with nitrogen and one without, and observed how they grew and developed over time. In the end, we found that nitrogen acted as a fertilizer for the plants' growth.</p>

<div class="slide slide-textphotos">
  <div class="slide-textphotos-text">
    <p>We had a wide range of projects and activities, but the ones that stood out most to me were programming, machine learning, design, and electronics.</p>
    <p>At the end, we created a scientific research poster to showcase what projects we'd been working on that summer to the people at the lab. I sadly lost my poster, but I found a picture of it on their website.</p>

<div class="slide slide-textphotos">
  <div class="slide-textphotos-text">
    <p>At the end of the program, we had a closing conference where we met faculty and all the other interns working across Berkeley Lab, a great chance to see the range of research happening at the lab.</p>
    <p>We also toured many of the buildings, which is where I first got a real sense of how interesting the ALS was, and that ended up being a big reason I wanted to work there.</p>

<div class="slide slide-textphotos">
  <div class="slide-textphotos-text">
    <p>After the program, I realized the importance of being given a chance to explore the fields of STEM. Where I come from, it's not always easy to get that kind of experience, especially for students from underrepresented communities, so I wanted to give back to students in a similar position.</p>
    <p>I went to my old elementary school and taught the students the fundamentals of programming using Scratch, and CAD using Tinkercad!</p>

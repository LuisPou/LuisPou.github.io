### Hi, I'm Luis

I am a first-generation Mexican American student at UC Berkeley, and a Regents and Chancellor's Scholar.

I grew up speaking only Spanish, helping and watching my dad build things with his hands as a carpenter. That combination pushed me toward science and creation and eventually, engineering.

I love learning about automation, robotics, machine learning, AI, embedded systems, controls, systems engineering, and mechanical design. I'm especially interested in how hardware and software can come together to create intelligent, autonomous systems.
